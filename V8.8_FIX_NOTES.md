# Personal Healthcare v8.8 – Health Risk & Action Plan

- Added Health Risk & Action Plan grouped into cardiovascular, metabolic/diabetes, kidney and data-quality domains.
- Added prioritized next actions instead of a single opaque numeric health score.
- Added Historical Comparison for blood pressure, BMI, weight and repeated lab results.
- Prevents same-day imported weight/BMI values from being labelled as a real long-term trend.
- Reduced duplication: Health Flags is now Data Quality & Monitoring.
- Home BP plan follows NICE HBPM method: morning/evening, 2 readings at least 1 minute apart, at least 4 days (ideally 7), discard day 1 for the average.
- Glucose interpretation uses CDC screening thresholds; lipid context uses NHS healthy guides; kidney interpretation avoids diagnosing CKD from one eGFR result.
- Does not calculate a numeric cardiovascular risk score until required demographic/clinical variables are available.
- For decision support only; not a diagnosis or substitute for clinician review.
