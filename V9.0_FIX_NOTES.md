# Personal Healthcare v9.0 — Personalised Guidance

## Added
- Personalised **Do / Limit / Avoid** guidance tied to the user's latest health data.
- Reasons are shown under every recommendation (BMI, blood pressure, glucose/HbA1c, lipids, uric acid).
- Medication Safety panel with conservative rules for simvastatin, amlodipine and allopurinol.
- Health Goals and Monitoring Plan.

## Safety design
- Uses “avoid” only when evidence/medication-specific advice supports it; otherwise prefers “limit”.
- Never tells the user to stop or change a medicine by themselves.
- Does not diagnose gout from uric acid alone or diabetes from one app reading.
- Does not assign a universal LDL treatment target without cardiovascular-risk context.
- Reminds users that kidney/heart fluid restrictions may override generic hydration advice.

## Guideline basis used in rules
- NICE NG136: home blood-pressure monitoring and lifestyle advice for hypertension.
- NHS: high cholesterol / saturated-fat advice; medicine-specific food interactions.
- CDC: diabetes testing ranges and Diabetes Prevention Program lifestyle targets.
- NHS gout dietary guidance: alcohol, hydration and high-purine food considerations.
